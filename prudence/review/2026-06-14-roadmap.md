# Prudence PWA — Make-It-Amazing Roadmap

_Multi-agent review, 2026-06-14. 48 ideas → 37 survived curation (on-brand, not already present, worth the effort)._

## Vision

Prudence should be the place where end-to-end encryption stops being a marketing line and becomes
something you can *see, touch, and trust in ten seconds*. A first-time visitor pairs by QR, sends a
message, taps it, and watches the relay receive only gibberish — that single moment converts
skeptics into believers. At the same time, every handler and helper in the codebase is a clean,
anchored, copy-pasteable lesson, so the integrator reading App.tsx learns the *right* way to wire the
SDK, not just *a* way.

---

## Top 3 — Do These First

1. **Relay Ciphertext Peek** — The single most visceral proof of the entire product promise; turns
   "trust us" into "see for yourself" in one tap, at medium effort.
2. **Conversation list + per-conversation search** — Highest impact-to-effort UX gap in the whole
   app; both are small, client-side, and table-stakes for feeling like a real messenger.
3. **Account backup/restore** — Without it, clearing browser storage means permanent account loss;
   the line between "neat demo" and "production-ready," and the SDK crypto already exists.

## Feature Gaps

| Title | What + Why | Impact | Effort | Files |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Per-conversation message search** ⭐ | Messages already hydrated client-side; in-memory filter + highlight. | high | s | `Thread.tsx` |
| **Account backup/restore** ⭐ | Clearing storage = permanent account loss; SDK `exportIdentity`/`importIdentity` do the crypto. | high | m | `Settings.tsx` (new), `App.tsx` |
| **Message-level deletion** | SDK `deleteMessage` is implemented/tested; Prudence only deletes whole conversations. | high | s | `Thread.tsx`, `App.tsx` |
| **Conversation import/restore from archive blob** | `importArchive` unused; standalone restore seeds a new device fast. | high | m | `Login.tsx`, `App.tsx` |
| **Bulk conversation export** | Only per-conversation export exists; `exportAllConversationsInstance` for full backup. | high | m | `Settings.tsx`, `App.tsx` |
| **Presence indicators** | SDK fires `onPresence`/`getPresence`; Prudence ignores it. Online/offline dots. | medium | s | `App.tsx`, `ConversationList.tsx`, `Thread.tsx` |
| **Contact introductions** | SDK `introduceContacts` enables organic mesh growth; no UI. | medium | l | `ContactDetail.tsx` (new), `App.tsx` |

**De-dup note:** a shared **Settings modal** is the natural home for backup/restore, bulk export,
typing toggle, relay picker, and the identity card — build it once (with backup/restore) and the rest
dock into it.
